Cookie Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

What are cookies?

Cookies are small files that a website can store on your computer or phone, often to remember you or to track your visits.

Cookies on this website

This website does not set cookies. We do not use analytics, advertising, tracking or social media plug-ins. The notice asks whether you accept or decline this policy; your choice is saved in your browser's local storage so we do not ask again on each visit. It stays on your device until you clear your browser data, is only used to remember your response, and is not sent to us. Choose “Cookie settings” in the footer to change your response. If local storage is unavailable, the notice may appear again on another page or visit.

Links to other websites

Our pages link to our Facebook, X and LinkedIn pages. If you follow one of those links, that website may set its own cookies, which are covered by its own cookie policy, not ours.

More information

To find out how we handle personal information, see our Privacy Policy. If you have any questions, email info@netsafeit.co.uk.
