What we do

Six services that cover the basics most small businesses are missing. Take one, or let us look after all of them.

Cyber security risk assessment

A thorough review of your computers, network and cloud accounts to find weaknesses, with a clear, prioritised list of what to fix.

Staff cyber security training

Short, practical sessions that teach your team to spot phishing emails, scams and other threats before they do damage.

Data backup and recovery

Reliable backups and a tested recovery plan, so a failed laptop, ransomware or a simple mistake doesn't stop your business.

Firewall installation and management

Set-up and ongoing management of firewalls to keep unwanted visitors out of your network.

Software updates and patching

Regular updates to your operating systems and applications to close the security holes attackers rely on.

Password management

Sensible password policies, password managers and multi-factor authentication to stop unauthorised access to your accounts.
